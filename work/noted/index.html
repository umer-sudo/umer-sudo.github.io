The problemBefore we started

An audience

115K people, consistent attention, and no product. Brand deals paid inconsistently and every merch idea died at the question nobody had answered: what does it cost to make, what does it sell for, and what is left.

What we did4 moves

What we did, in order.

  1. 01

    The concept

    A full identity built on a red-pen annotation idea — a brand, not a logo on a shirt.

  2. 02

    Margin first

    An eight-product line priced against real production costs, with the margin table built before anything was designed.

  3. 03

    Production

    Printful and Tapstitch hybrid, so quality and turnaround both hold at volume.

  4. 04

    The launch

    Instagram launch strategy, content calendar, and a handoff package so the cycle runs without us.
