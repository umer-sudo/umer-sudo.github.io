A merch brand built from a red pen and an annotation concept.

Client — Julia · @theonlycanadianbaconDiscipline — Creator growth · BrandTimeline — Seven months, ongoingOutcome — 3K new followers a month
01 · The problem

An audiencewith nothingto buy.

115K people, consistent attention, and no product. Brand deals paid inconsistently and every merch idea died at the question nobody had answered: what does it cost to make, what does it sell for, and what is left.

02 · What we did
  1. 01The conceptA full identity built on a red-pen annotation idea — a brand, not a logo on a shirt.
  2. 02Margin firstAn eight-product line priced against real production costs, with the margin table built before anything was designed.
  3. 03ProductionPrintful and Tapstitch hybrid, so quality and turnaround both hold at volume.
  4. 04The launchInstagram launch strategy, content calendar, and a handoff package so the cycle runs without us.
