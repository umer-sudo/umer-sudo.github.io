Verdict

It’s live.

For a buyer, live means this: browse it, offer on it, pay into a hold.

Karachi’s thrift trade used to run on comment sections and trust. Now a buyer’s money waits with ehsaan until the parcel is opened and approved.

The Now Calling board on ehsaanapp.com is the door. It lets people in a few at a time.

Every hold, payout and refund is a new line in a ledger nobody can edit. Payouts and refunds run on their own.
