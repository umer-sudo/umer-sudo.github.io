ehsaan

Work / Pakistan · thrift marketplace app

An app where people sell the clothes they no longer wear.

Product · money engine · brand · launch

The problem

When strangers trade, the hard part is the money.

Second-hand clothing in Pakistan moved through comment sections, WhatsApp groups and DM haggling. No listings, no search, and nothing to stop a buyer paying for a jacket that never arrives.

A thrift app is easy to draw. A thrift app that holds a stranger's money safely, and lets go of it at exactly the right moment, is the real product.

What we built

The whole product: the app, the money engine underneath, the brand and the launch.

  • The marketplace app

    Listing, browsing, offers and orders for buyers and sellers, plus a separate admin side to run it.

  • The money engine

    Payments held in escrow, a wallet ledger that can only be added to, a bank card gateway, and scheduled jobs for payouts and refunds.

  • A Gen-Z brand

    Identity, type and product design for people who already trade this way: fast, visual and not embarrassed about second-hand.

  • The launch

    A waitlist and a live Now Calling board that lets people in a few at a time, with social, video and street interviews made alongside the build.

  • How it is built

    React, Vite, TypeScript and Supabase through Lovable, then Claude Code working against a written knowledge base, so the product keeps its own rules as it grows.

The money path

Follow one payment from card to wallet.

Scroll, and the path draws itself: who holds the money, when it moves, and what is written down each time.

  1. The buyer pays by card.The payment comes in through a bank card gateway.
  2. ehsaan holds it.The money sits in escrow. The seller can see it is there. Nobody can spend it yet.
  3. Every move is written down.A wallet ledger that only ever gains lines. Nothing is edited, nothing is deleted.
  4. Parcel opened, item right.The buyer says yes, and a scheduled job pays the seller's wallet.
  5. Item not right?The refund job sends the money back, and the ledger gets a new line rather than a rubbed-out old one.
  6. Checked before anyone paid.The whole path had a money-safety audit before the closed beta opened.

The hard parts

Money that belongs to two strangers at once has to be handled like glass.

  • Escrow

    The buyer pays, but the seller cannot touch it yet. The money waits until the parcel is opened and approved.

  • A ledger you can only add to

    Every hold, payout and refund is a new line. Nothing is ever edited or deleted, so the history always adds up and a mistake is fixed in the open.

  • Jobs that run on their own

    Payouts and refunds run on a schedule, so nobody has to remember to press a button.

  • Checked before anyone paid

    A money-safety audit of the whole path ran before the closed beta opened.

Where it is now

It is live, and opening a few people at a time.

Waitlist first, then a closed beta. The Now Calling board on ehsaanapp.com is the door, and it lets people in a few at a time.
