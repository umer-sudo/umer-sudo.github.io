Legacy Blinds

Which one
costs more?

A six-frame comparison carousel for a window-treatments brand.

ClientLegacy Blinds
DisciplinePerformance media · Creative
Timeline2026
Outcome[METRICS PENDING]
The challenge

Two products
that look alike
and price apart.

Linen roller shades and faux wood blinds photograph similarly and sell to the same room, so a scrolling buyer reads them as interchangeable and sorts on price alone. An ad that simply shows the product loses that comparison before it starts.

What we did

3 phases.

01

Ask, don’t tell

The carousel opens on a question — which of these costs more — so the first frame buys a swipe instead of asking for a click.

02

One product per frame

A, then A in detail, then B. Each frame carries a single claim about make and material, so the comparison is legible at thumb speed.

03

Land on craft, not price

The payoff frame reframes the question it opened with: style is one thing, craftsmanship is another. The buyer arrives at the link having already argued themselves out of sorting on price.

What we found

Answering the price question directly is what earns the swipe. A carousel that opens on a claim gets scrolled past; one that opens on a question gets finished.
