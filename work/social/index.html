The challenge

Reach without
an audience
to start from.

Four brands, small follower counts, and a category where reach is normally bought. The work was to make content that travels on its own and only pay where paying is efficient.

What we did

2 phases.

01

One operator, four accounts

Strategy, production and client communication in one pair of hands — no coordination layer. Two of the four ran under Drifted; two were led while Creative Strategist at ForjWell.

02

Organic first, paid where it pays

80% of the Poster Project’s 2.2M views were organic. BamBam’s paid traffic, led at ForjWell, ran at $0.25 per landing page view.

What we found

Reach beats followers. The Poster Project reached 199,392 accounts in 90 days — up 174.5% — from an 811-follower base.
