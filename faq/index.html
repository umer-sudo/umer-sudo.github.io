Open a question. Each answer ends with where to go next.

Q01

What does it actually cost?

Money

Two published monthly fees — $2,000 for one discipline, $4,000 for the full content and findability loop — or a tailored fee priced to your revenue target on the first call, out loud, before any contract exists. Advertising spend is separate. If the maths does not work for you, we will say so and point you somewhere cheaper.

Q02

How fast until we see anything?

Speed

Work starts in week one and the first creative is live in week three. Numbers by week four. The check-in cadence is written into the contract beside the target, so you know when you will hear from us before you sign anything.

Q03

How do we know it is working?

Proof

Every figure we report names its client and carries a receipt. The Gallery gets $5.20–$6 back per $1 of ad spend, every campaign to July 2026. That is published, not claimed — and where a number is not measured yet, the page says pending rather than guessing.

Is the AI work snake oil?

AI

Sometimes, elsewhere. Here it is a typist with perfect stamina: 1,250 auction listings written from photographs, 400+ hours returned to a real team, every listing checked by a person. The machine does volume; people do judgement.

Exit

Thirty days’ notice. You keep everything we made — files, accounts, ad history, the lot. If the work does not hold you, a clause should not have to.

Are we too small for you?

Size

The Poster Project had 811 followers at the time and reached 2.2M views in ninety days, 80% of it organic. Size is not the test. A clear number and a date are, and the founder signs off both on the first call.

Who actually does the work?

People

The people in the studio photograph. There is no account layer and no handoff to someone junior at signature. When you call, a person who touched your work answers — that is structural, not a promise.

Why “Drifted”?

Name

Because we drifted from how agencies are supposed to run — the theatre, the bloat, the invented metrics — and kept only the craft. The name is the pitch; the site is the evidence.

Can a Karachi agency work New York hours?

Hours

Yes — it is how we work. Drifted's team is in Karachi and works the New York business day, so you message during your day and hear back during your day. You never wait for Karachi to wake up, and the calls sit in your calendar, not ours.
