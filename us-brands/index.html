FAQ
What hours does Drifted work for US clients?

New York business hours, worked from Karachi. Calls are booked in your time zone and sit in your calendar, not ours, and the people on them are the people doing the work. You never wait for Karachi to wake up.

What does it cost a US brand?

The same published fees as everyone: two monthly fees in US dollars, listed on the pricing page, or a tailored fee priced to your revenue target on the first call. Advertising spend is separate and stays in your own account.

Whose ad account is it?

Yours. Your budget sits in your own Meta and Google accounts, and you can see every dollar of it. We never take a percentage of spend, because a fee that grows when your budget grows pays us to spend more rather than to spend well.

What if we want to stop?

Thirty days’ notice. The accounts, pages and creative are already yours because they were never in our name.
