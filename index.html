An app where people sell the clothes they no longer wear.

When strangers trade, the hard part is the money: who holds it, and when it moves.

The whole product: the app, the brand and the launch. Underneath it, the money path we built: payments held in escrow, a wallet ledger that can only be added to, card payments, and scheduled payouts and refunds, all checked for money safety before the beta.

The Netherlands · militaria shop

Oorlogsspullen

A shop that sells pieces of military history, and every piece needs describing.

Each item needs seven pieces of text, in Dutch and in English, in the shop's own voice.

A Write with AI button inside their own shop. Upload the photos and it drafts the title, both descriptions, the alt text and the search text, ready to edit before anything goes live. It drafts; a person still checks. The host's own sysadmin reviewed the code, and it was signed and installed inside a week.
