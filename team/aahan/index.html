■ 02AboutAahan

Who Aahan is.

Aahan was hired outright in September 2026, three days after he first walked in. The brief then was simple: be the face of Drifted's content and bring the business in through it.

In October he became Head of Creative. He now runs the team that makes everything a client sees: Khizer on edit, Sana on strategy and Ali on animation.

■ 03What they doThe job, verb first

The job.

  1. 01Runs the creative team: briefs, shoots, edits and how every account looks.
  2. 02Writes the team's briefs and approves their work before it goes to the founder for the final yes.
  3. 03Makes reels, carousels, statics, demo websites and client decks.
  4. 04Owns the client relationship between the people who make the work and the people who manage the account.
  5. 05Fronts Drifted's own content on camera.
  6. 06Presses publish on staging links once a piece is approved.

■ 05How they workProcess and tools

How Aahan works.

ARI builds, he directs. His agent turns a direction into frames, pages and decks fast enough that he can try three ideas where most people would try one.

The look of Drifted's own feed comes from him and ARI: high-contrast photographs, magazine type and glowing three-dimensional scenes. Directions come to him in threes, one of them risky, and he picks.

■ 06A day at DriftedNew York hours, from Karachi

A day at Drifted.

  1. Evening in Karachi, morning in New York

    Briefs out: who is making what today, and what good looks like.

  2. The middle of the shift

    Making, with ARI, and reviewing what the team has sent up.

  3. Late in Karachi

    Approvals, client decks, and anything that needs his face on camera.

  4. Before logging off

    The daily report: what got made, where it got stuck, what to do differently, what is needed tomorrow. On Friday, the weekly check-in.
