■ 06House rulesOpinions we would defend in public

Four rules, written down.

  1. R–01

    Say the number.

    Pick one, put a date on it, and write it into the contract.

  2. R–02

    Retainers should be losable.

    Thirty days’ notice. If we are not worth it, you should not be trapped.

  3. R–03

    No guaranteed results.

    We say what we think we can hit and report honestly — including when we miss.

  4. R–04

    We are not the cheap option.

    And we do not pretend to be. The work is priced where it is worth doing.
