UmerUmer Tariq · Founder · Drifted Marketing · Karachi

I’m 21. I started at 17, selling online before I knew what any of it was called. Then I closed 29 contracts inside three agencies and watched every client turn into a green or a red cell on somebody’s sheet. Nobody in the room knew their names.

Drifted is the opposite of that room. The people who pitch the work do the work. One number and one date go in the contract, and the number on the page is a number we can produce the account for.

I write everything in the journal — how to read an ad account, what a creator product line should cost, what “AI-native” is actually worth. It is the method, given away, because the method was never the hard part.
