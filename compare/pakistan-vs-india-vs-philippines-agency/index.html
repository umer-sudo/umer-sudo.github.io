■ 01The comparisonThe short answer

Pakistan, India or the Philippines

Pick the Philippines when you need voices on the phone through the American night. Pick India when you need a bench of specialists at a scale no single studio can match. Pick Pakistan when you want senior people on one account for a lower fee, in a studio that keeps your hours. We are the third, and the checks below apply to all three, including us.

When is the Philippines the better choice?

When the work is a voice. Customer support, order confirmation calls, live chat through the American night: the Philippine industry grew up around exactly this, the spoken English carries an American ear, and the shifts already exist. If you are buying coverage, a person awake and answering while you sleep, the Philippines does it better than Pakistan or India, and we would say so. It is also the stronger choice when your brand voice is conversational American and the words will be spoken aloud rather than read.

When is India the better choice?

When you need depth and scale at once. India has the largest pool of specialists in the region, and a large Indian agency can put an analyst, a feed specialist and a developer on your account without strain, as no studio in Karachi or Manila can. If you run campaigns across many markets, need an engineering bench of dozens, or want a named specialist for every discipline, India is the honest answer. What you trade for it is layers: the people who pitch are rarely the people who do the work, and the account manager between you is part of the fee.

When does Pakistan make sense?

When the account is one brand with one number and what you want is senior people reading it every week rather than a floor of juniors. The Pakistani industry is younger and smaller than India’s, which cuts both ways: fewer famous names to point to, fewer layers between you and the work, and a fee that buys the founder’s attention rather than a coordinator’s. It is also the obvious choice when you sell inside Pakistan, where the inbox, cash on delivery and the courier are the whole funnel. We are in Karachi, keep New York hours, and run exactly that kind of account.

What to check, whatever the flag

  1. 01

    Time the reply

    Message the agency at the end of its stated overlap window and note when the answer arrives. Every country has agencies that keep their window and agencies that promise it.

  2. 02

    Read the copy as a customer

    Pay for one short brief and judge it as the buyer would, not as the client. Fluent English and English for your market are different skills in all three countries.

  3. 03

    Ask who leaves and what stays

    Not the retention rate, which nobody answers badly. Ask what a replacement would receive on their first morning, and ask to see it.

  4. 04

    Find out who holds the spend

    Ad budget routed through an agency abroad is money you cannot easily recover. It goes from your card to the platform, or you walk.

  5. 05

    Price the exit

    Monthly billing, thirty days’ notice, accounts in your name. If leaving costs more than a month, the geography is the least of your risks.

■ 04QuestionsAnswered before the call

The questions that follow.

FAQ
Is Pakistan cheaper than India for an agency?

Broadly the same band, and sometimes lower for senior time. The saving is never the reason to choose any of the three; the fee buys attention, and attention is what to compare.

Which country has the best English for US copy?

The Philippines for spoken, American-inflected English; India and Pakistan for written English in the British habit. None of that replaces a paid test brief judged by someone from your market.

Do you keep US hours from Karachi?

Yes. The New York working day, written into the contract with the days it applies, so review calls happen inside your day rather than at the edge of ours.

What if I need coverage around the clock?

Then you need a support operation, and the Philippines is the better place to look for it. We run marketing on your working day, not a help desk through the night.

Who owns the accounts and the work?

You do, in any of the three countries if the contract says so, and with us it always does. Ask before signing rather than after.
