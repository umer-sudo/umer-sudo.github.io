30:["$","p","5",{"children":"There is a second reason, less discussed. A hidden fee protects an agency from its competitors. If nobody publishes, nobody has to defend a number against a rival's number, and the comparison the buyer most wants to make is the one the buyer cannot make."}]

A founder who cannot audit the work, and most cannot, has lost the one thing they could have audited: the price."}]
