Daraz or your own Shopify store: where a Pakistani brand should sell

What a marketplace gives you and what it takes, what your own store costs to run each month, and why most growing brands in Pakistan end up using both.

By Umer Tariq, founderPublished 17 November 20264 min read

Every Pakistani brand that starts selling online meets the same choice. Daraz has the buyers already: people who open the app to shop, trust its delivery and returns, and pay the way they like. Your own store, on Shopify or something like it, has none of that on day one, but it keeps the customer, the margin and the brand. The answer is rarely one or the other forever. It is which comes first, and what each is for.

The short version

Sell on Daraz when you need buyers now and your product can compete on price and reviews there. Build your own store when you want to own the customer, the brand and the margin, and you can bring your own traffic with Instagram, Facebook and search. Most growing brands use Daraz for reach and their own store for repeat buyers.

What does each one cost?

CostDarazYour own Shopify store
To startSeller registration; little or no upfront costA monthly Shopify plan, a theme, a domain
Per saleCommission by category, payment and handling feesPayment gateway fees on card and wallet orders
To get buyersMarketplace ads and campaigns, optional but often neededYour own ads, content and search work
Running costsPromotions the platform asks sellers to joinApps, the courier integration, someone to run it
Who owns the customerThe marketplaceYou

Daraz publishes its commission rates by category inside Seller Center, and they change, so read the current table for your category rather than a blog's figure. Shopify's plans are priced in dollars and billed monthly; on top of the plan, budget for the apps you add, a Pakistani payment gateway or wallet, and the courier connection. Our piece at /blog/what-a-shopify-store-costs-in-pakistan breaks that down line by line.

When does Daraz make more sense?

  • You are testing whether a product sells at all and do not want to build a store first.
  • Your product is a known category that people search for by name, where price and reviews decide.
  • You do not yet have the content or ad budget to bring buyers to a store of your own.
  • You want the marketplace's delivery and returns to handle trust for you.

When does your own store make more sense?

  • Your brand, look and story are part of what people pay for.
  • You sell through Instagram and Facebook already and want somewhere better to send buyers than a direct message.
  • You want the customer's details so you can sell to them again by message or email.
  • Your margin cannot carry a marketplace commission plus a discount campaign.
  • You plan to sell abroad as well as in Pakistan.

A store does not bring buyers by itself. If you build one, plan how people will find it: ads, content made for the people scrolling past, and the search basics in /blog/shopify-seo-basics-for-a-new-store. A beautiful store with no traffic is a shop on an empty street.

Can a brand use both?

Most successful ones do. The marketplace brings first-time buyers who would never have found the brand, and the packaging, the insert card and the follow-up message invite them to buy next time from the brand's own store or Instagram. Keep prices consistent, so neither channel undercuts the other, and keep stock counts in one place so you do not sell the same item twice.

When is neither the answer?

For some small sellers, selling directly through Instagram and WhatsApp is the right place to stay for a while: no commission, no monthly plan, and a direct conversation with each buyer. It stops working when the messages become too many to answer and orders get lost, which is the moment a store earns its keep. We build stores for brands at that point; if you are not there yet, you do not need us.

Questions we get asked

How much does Shopify cost per month in Pakistan?

Shopify bills its plans monthly in dollars. Add the apps you install, a local payment gateway's fees and your courier integration to get the real monthly cost.

Is Daraz commission the same for every product?

No. It varies by category and changes over time. Check the current rates in Daraz Seller Center for your category before you price.

Can I accept cash on delivery on Shopify in Pakistan?

Yes. Cash on delivery can be offered as a payment method, and Pakistani couriers connect to Shopify through apps or their own integrations.

Should I start on Daraz and move to my own store later?

That is a sensible order for many brands: prove the product sells, then build a store once you have content and a way to bring buyers to it.
