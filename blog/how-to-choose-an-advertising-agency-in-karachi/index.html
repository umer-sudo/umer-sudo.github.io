How to choose an advertising agency in Karachi

What separates the Karachi agencies worth a meeting from the ones that will waste a quarter, the questions that tell them apart, and what each kind is actually good at.

By Umer Tariq, founderPublished 17 November 20265 min read

Karachi has more advertising agencies than any other city in Pakistan, and the range runs from the networks that make television campaigns for banks and telecoms to two-person studios that run Instagram ads for a clothing label. Searching for the best one is the wrong question. The useful question is which kind of agency fits what you are trying to sell, and then which of that kind will actually do the work.

The short version

Decide first whether you need brand advertising (television, outdoor, a campaign idea) or performance work (ads that are judged on sales). Then shortlist two or three agencies of that kind, ask who will touch your account every week, ask to see an account they run today, and choose the one that names a number and a date in writing.

Most small and medium brands do not need a network agency. They need someone who can make content that stops a thumb and then run the ads that put it in front of buyers, measured on what sold. That combination is rarer than the agency lists suggest, because content shops and ad shops are often different companies that blame each other when the month goes badly.

What should you ask before you sign?

  1. Who will work on my account every week, by name, and how many other accounts do they carry?
  2. Can I see an ad account you run today, with the client's permission, and what you changed in it last month?
  3. Whose name is on the ad account and the page: mine or yours? If we part, what do I keep?
  4. How are you paid: a flat fee, a share of my ad spend, or both? What happens to your fee if my spend doubles?
  5. What number will you be judged on, and by what date?
  6. What is the notice period, and can I give it by email at any time?

The answers that should worry you are vague ones. An agency that cannot name the person on your account usually has not decided who it will be. An agency that wants the ad account in its own name is building a reason for you never to leave. And an agency that will not name a number is telling you, politely, that it does not expect to be held to one. Our longer list of questions is at /tools/questions-to-ask-an-agency, and the contract clauses to check are at /tools/agency-contract-checklist.

Does it matter whether the agency is in Karachi?

For a Karachi shop, restaurant or clinic, it helps. Someone who can visit, shoot on site and understands which neighbourhoods buy what will make better local ads than someone who has never been to Clifton or Tariq Road. For a brand selling across Pakistan or abroad, the address matters much less than the hours, the language of the team and whether they have run ads in your market before.

We are a Karachi studio that works New York business hours, so we answer that question from both sides. Local brands get people who can be in the room; American and European brands get a team awake in their working day. The full picture is on /pakistan/marketing-agency-karachi and /us-brands.

How much does an advertising agency in Karachi cost?

Prices vary more than the work does, because agencies price in different ways: a monthly retainer, a share of ad spend, a fee per post or per video, or a project price for a campaign. Compare them on the same footing by adding up what you would pay in a typical month, including the ad spend itself, and asking what you get for it in deliverables and hours. Our piece on how retainers are structured, at /blog/how-agency-retainer-fees-work, walks through the arithmetic, and our own monthly fees are published on /pricing.

When is an agency the wrong answer?

When you have nothing to sell yet, or nothing to say. An agency cannot fix a product nobody wants or a price that does not work, and it will happily take a fee while it tries. It is also the wrong answer when one good freelancer could do the job: a single channel, a small budget and an owner who can review the work every week. Hire an agency when the work needs several skills at once and someone accountable for the result.

Questions we get asked

What is the best advertising agency in Karachi?

There is no single best one, because the agencies do different jobs. The best one for you is the one that does the kind of work you need, names who will do it, and agrees in writing what it will be judged on.

Should I choose a big agency or a small one?

Choose by who does the work. A big agency gives you depth and process; a small one usually gives you the senior people every week. Ask each who touches your account and how often.

Can a Karachi agency run ads for a business abroad?

Yes. Meta and Google ads are run from anywhere. What matters is that the team has run ads in your market, works hours that overlap with yours, and keeps the ad account in your name.

Who should own the ad account?

You should. The account, the page and the data belong to the business. A good agency works inside your account with access you can remove.
