38:["$","p","4",{"children":"The self-interested one first, so it is out of the way. Managing a small account costs an agency almost as much as managing a large one. 40:["$","h2","12",{"id":"floor","className":"disp","children":"So what is the practical floor?"}]
41:["$","p","13",{"children":"Around $5,000 a month of media is where most of the arithmetic starts working, and there is a second reason for that number beyond the learning phase. Stackmatix's 2026 guide puts market fees at 10 to 20 per cent of spend, or flat retainers from roughly $500 to past $5,000, with small-business engagements more often between $1,000 and $2,500. At $5,000 of media a $1,500 fee is already 30 per cent of the total. Below that, you are paying more to manage the money than the money can plausibly return."}]
