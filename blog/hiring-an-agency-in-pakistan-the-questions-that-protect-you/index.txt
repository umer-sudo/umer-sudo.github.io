39:["$","p","3",{"children":"The advertising account, the business page, the website, the domain and the tracking all belong in your name, in your own business manager, with the agency added as a partner. That is the single most important line in any agreement and the one most often missing."}]
3a:["$","p","4",{"children":"The common arrangement here is the reverse: the agency runs your campaigns inside its own account, for a reason that sounds practical. Paying a platform in dollars from Pakistan is awkward, the agency already has a card that works, and it is simpler for everyone. It is simpler right up until you leave, when you discover that the account, the audiences it learned, the tracking data and every result it ever produced belong to someone else. You start again from nothing, and the next agency starts from nothing too."}]
3b:["$","p","5",{"children":"Ask the question plainly: at the end of this contract, what do I log in to, and what do I keep? If the answer involves exporting a report rather than keeping an account, the price is higher than the fee."}]

3d:["$","p","7",{"children":"Two payments should never be mixed: the fee you pay the agency and the money you spend on ads. The spend goes to the platform, from your card or your business manager, and the agency never touches it. The fee is a flat figure for the work, agreed in advance."}]
3e:["$","p","8",{"children":"Watch for the fee quoted as a share of spend. It is common, it sounds proportionate, and it rewards the agency for spending more rather than for selling more. If an agency must run spend on its own card because of how you pay, insist on the platform’s own receipts, every month, with no rounding."}]
