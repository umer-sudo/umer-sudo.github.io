39:["$","p","3",{"children":"The object is the content. The product, the room, the process, the thing in the case. A brand that sells towels films the towel being made, folded, used. 3a:["$","p","4",{"children":"This is the lesson from the short films we make for Gettysburg Museum of History. 3d:["$","p","7",{"children":"A short video is not a shorter long video. It is one idea, delivered in one movement. The beat is the unit: a cut, a line, a reveal. The ending is where most videos go wrong, because the maker keeps going after the idea has landed. Cut on the idea."}]
