Shopify SEO: the fixes that actually matter for a small store

What Shopify already does for search, the handful of settings and pages it leaves to you, and the app habits that quietly slow a store down.

The short versionShopify already builds the sitemap, the robots file and canonical links for you. What it leaves to you is the words: a unique title and description for every product and collection, product descriptions written for a buyer rather than copied from a supplier, image alt text, collection pages with a paragraph of real copy, and fewer apps. Do those, connect Search Console, and most small stores are ahead of their competitors.
