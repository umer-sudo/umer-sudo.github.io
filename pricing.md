# Drifted Marketing — pricing

> Published fees, in US dollars. Advertising spend is separate, stays in the client's own account, and is never charged as a percentage. Source: https://driftedmarketing.com/pricing/

## Starter — $2,000 a month

One channel, one number, a brand testing whether any of this works.

Includes:
- Ads: Meta ads on one account, four new ad creatives a month, optimised weekly
- Or content: four short videos and three carousels a month
- Or search: four page optimisations, one researched article and Google Business Profile posts a month
- One of the three, picked on the first call — not all three
- A monthly written report and a monthly call

Not included:
- Advertising spend, which is yours and stays in your account

## Standard — $4,000 a month

A brand that wants the whole loop running — content, findability and counsel.

Includes:
- Eight short videos and six carousels a month
- Four static posters a month
- Eight page optimisations a month
- Two researched articles a month, eight hundred to twelve hundred words each
- Answer-engine entries, so the brand is quotable to an assistant
- Google Business Profile maintained, with four posts a month
- Two email campaigns a month
- A visibility report, a written monthly report and a monthly call
- Four consultation hours a month

Not included:
- Paid advertising management and advertising spend
- On-site photography and videography
- Website development

## Tailored — priced to the number

Paid media at scale, a product build, a shoot — or all three at once.

Includes:
- Paid media management across Meta and Google
- Custom AI product, scoped against how it earns before it is built
- Photography, video and production
- Everything in Standard

Not included:
- Advertising spend, which is yours and stays in your account

## Terms

- Thirty days' notice to leave; the client keeps the accounts and the work.
- Work starts in week one; the first creative is live in week three; numbers by week four.
