A Karachi marketing agency that says the number

Drifted Marketing is a growth agency at 111-C, Murtaza Commercial, DHA Phase 8, Karachi. In detail

What does a marketing agency in Karachi do for a business like yours?

It takes one number you care about, usually orders or enquiries, and builds the shortest route to it. For most Karachi brands that is Meta ads that start conversations, short video that gets watched rather than skipped, and pages that come up when someone searches for what you sell. We make the creative, buy the media, read the account every week and send you the figures in plain English. What you should not get is a logo refresh you did not ask for, a deck full of stock photography, or a report that hides the one number you wanted.

Why does a Karachi agency keep New York hours?

Because our clients are in the United States, the Netherlands, Australia and Pakistan, and the studio runs on one clock rather than four. The practical effect for a Karachi business is simple. We are at our desks through your afternoon and evening, which is when your customers are on Instagram and when the orders come in. The practical effect for a buyer abroad is that a question sent from Pennsylvania at ten in the morning gets answered in the same working day, not the next one.

How the first month runs

  1. Week one: the number

    We get into the ad account, the page and the analytics, and agree in writing what success looks like by the end of the quarter. If the number is not realistic we say so before you pay for a second month.

  2. Week two: the work starts

    Creative gets written, shot and cut here in DHA. You see every piece before it runs, and you see the plan for where it runs.

  3. Week three: live

    The first ads and posts go out. We optimise weekly, cut what loses and rebuild what wins.

  4. Week four: the figures

    You get a written report that puts spend next to return, in rupees or dollars, with nothing hidden in a dashboard you need a login for.

What we will not do, in Karachi or anywhere

  • Guarantee a result. Anyone who guarantees a number has not looked at your account.
  • Take a percentage of your ad spend. The fee is flat, so we are paid to spend well, not to spend more.
  • Hold your accounts. The page, the ad account and the files stay in your name from the first day.
  • Pitch with a deck. The first call is a conversation about your number, not a presentation about us.
  • Pretend to be the cheap option. There is always someone cheaper, and you already know what that buys.

Proof from two Karachi brands

The Gallery sells personalised newborn wear, and in Pakistan that sale closes in a DM, not a checkout. We built ten creatives, pointed the campaign at the inbox and counted orders where they actually happened. Every campaign to July 2026 returned $5.20–$6 for every $1 of ads.

ehsaan is a person-to-person thrift marketplace for a category that lived in Instagram comments and WhatsApp groups. Is Drifted the cheap marketing agency in Karachi?

No. There are agencies in Karachi that will run your ads for a fraction of our fee, and some of them are fine. What the fee buys here is senior attention: the people who pitch the work do the work, the creative is made for your account rather than adapted from someone else’s, and the number is reported every week whether it is good or bad. If price is the only thing you are choosing on, you will find a cheaper bid in an afternoon, and we would rather you did than sign with us for the wrong reason.

FAQ
Where is Drifted’s office?

At 111-C, Murtaza Commercial, DHA Phase 8, Karachi. We meet clients there, on a call, or at your premises if the work needs it.

Do you only work with businesses in DHA?

No. We work across Karachi and Lahore, and most of our clients are abroad. The office is where the work is made, not a boundary on who we make it for.

What does a marketing agency in Karachi cost?

Ours costs $2,000 a month for one discipline or $4,000 a month for the full loop, with ad spend separate and in your own account. Other agencies in the city will quote you less; ask what the number includes.

Do you take a cut of the ad budget?

Never. A fee that grows with your spend pays an agency to spend more. Ours is flat, agreed before the first invoice.

Can we meet before we sign?

Yes. The first call is thirty minutes and there is no deck. If you are in Karachi, come to the office.
