In detail

How do you choose an SEO agency in Karachi?

Ask four things before you compare fees. Who holds the website and listing logins: it should be you. What exactly is done each month, as a count of pages and posts you can check. How results are reported: by the searches and enquiries that moved, not a ranking screenshot for a phrase nobody types. And whether they buy links, because a bought link is a penalty waiting to land on your domain. An agency that answers all four plainly is worth a call, whoever it is, including us.

Why does search in Karachi start with Google Maps?

Because most local searches in the city are typed on a phone with an area attached: a clinic in Clifton, a bakery in Bahadurabad, a salon near Tariq Road. Google answers those with a map and a few listings before any website, and it ranks the listings on category, completeness, reviews, photographs and how recently the business posted. A listing with the right category, a WhatsApp number, current hours and regular posts will usually beat a better website that has none of that.

How SEO runs for a Karachi business

  1. Read the listing and Search Console

    We start from what people already type to find you and where you appear today, in Google's own data rather than a keyword tool's estimate.

  2. Fix the listing

    Category, service areas, hours, photographs, the WhatsApp button and replies to past reviews. For a business with a door, this is the first week's work.

  3. Write the Karachi question list

    Every query worth a page, written the way Karachi buyers type it, including the area names. Each one gets one page.

  4. Rewrite the pages

    On Starter, four page optimisations, one researched article and Google Business posts every month. Standard doubles the pages and the articles.

  5. Report by search and enquiry

    One page a month: what you appear for, which pages moved, and the calls, chats and direction requests the listing produced.

What we will not sell you

  • A guaranteed first position on Google. Nobody outside Google controls one.
  • Fake reviews, or reviews traded for discounts. Google removes them and can suspend the listing.
  • Bought links, or link packages sold by the hundred.
  • A listing at an address you do not trade from. Those get suspended in Karachi as they do everywhere.
  • A report you need our login to read.

FAQ
How much does SEO cost in Karachi?

Ours is $2,000 a month for search on its own, or part of the $4,000 Standard loop. Cheaper quotes exist in the city; ask each one for the monthly count of pages and posts.

Can we meet at the office?

Yes. The studio is at 111-C, Murtaza Commercial, DHA Phase 8. The first call is thirty minutes and there is no deck.

What moves first?

The listing, then the rewritten pages, then the articles. The first report at the end of month one says honestly what moved and what did not.

Do you only work with businesses in DHA?

No. A business in Gulshan, Saddar or North Nazimabad gets the same method and the same fee, and so does one in Lahore.

Does SEO also help us show up in ChatGPT and AI answers?

Mostly, yes. Assistants draw on the same listing, reviews and pages Google reads, so a complete listing and pages that answer the question first serve both. Standard adds a monthly record of what the assistants say about you.

How long before SEO shows results in Karachi?

A fixed listing can start bringing calls and direction requests within weeks. Rewritten pages take longer, usually a few months, which is why the monthly report shows what moved and what has not yet.
