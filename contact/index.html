FAQ
What if we’re too small?

Then we’ll say so on the call rather than after a month of retainer. If the number you need can’t justify the fee, that is a fast no, not a slow one.

What does it cost?

Retainers start where the work is worth doing, and they’re losable at thirty days’ notice. We’ll give you a range on the first call once we know the number and the date.

Do we have to sign anything to talk?

No. Thirty minutes, no deck, no NDA to start. You leave with a yes, a no, or a not yet — and the reason, in writing.

Who’s actually on the call?

The people who would run the account. There is no account layer here, which means there is nobody to hand you off to afterwards.
