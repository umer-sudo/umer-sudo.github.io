We are the wrong call if…

Some people should not hire us. Better you hear it here than on the call. If none of this puts you off, the work is here and how it runs is here.

  • You want the cheapest possible price. There is always someone cheaper, and at some point that person is a person with a template.
  • You want a team of ten names on a slide. There is a small team here and the person who pitches the work does the work.
  • You want us to promise a number before we have seen the account. We will give you a number on the first call, out loud — but not before we have looked.
  • You need the whole thing done by Friday. Work starts in week one, the first creative is live in week three, and numbers land around week four.
  • You want to own none of it. The advertising account, the pages and the creative stay yours, and that means you have to hold the keys.

Questions

The questions that follow.

The money questions first, answered the way we answer them on the call.

How much does a performance marketing agency cost in Pakistan?

Drifted, a growth agency in Karachi, starts at the fee at the top of this page for one discipline. Anything larger, more channels, paid media at scale, a build or a shoot, is priced on the first call, out loud, before any contract exists. Advertising spend is separate and stays in your own account. Whatever you are quoted elsewhere, ask what the fee includes and whether it grows with your spend.

Is advertising spend included in the fee?

No. Your advertising budget is yours, it sits in your own account, and you can see every dollar of it. We never take a percentage of spend, because a fee that grows when your budget grows pays us to spend more rather than to spend well.

What happens if we want to stop?

Thirty days’ notice, and the accounts, pages and creative are already yours because they were never in our name. No minimum term, no auto-renewal you have to remember to cancel.

How fast until we see anything?

Work starts in week one and the first creative is live in week three. Numbers by week four. The check-in cadence is written into the contract beside the target, so you know when you will hear from us before you sign anything.

What happens on the first call?

Thirty minutes, no deck. You bring the number you need to hit and the date; we tell you on the call whether we can, what it would cost and what we would do first. If the answer is no, you hear that too, along with where we would go instead.
