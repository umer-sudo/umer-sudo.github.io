FAQ
What does it actually cost?

One monthly fee that covers performance, creative, build and counsel. We price it to your revenue target on the first call — out loud, with a number, before any contract exists. If the maths does not work for you, we will say so and point you somewhere cheaper.

How fast until we see anything?

Work in week one. Numbers by week four. We publish the check-in cadence in the contract, and a month with nothing to point at is treated as a failure — ours, not yours.

How do we know it is working?

Every figure we report names its client and carries a receipt. The Gallery gets $5.20–$6 back per $1 of ads, every campaign. That is published, not claimed — and where a number is not measured yet, the page says pending rather than guessing.

Is the AI work snake oil?

Sometimes, elsewhere. Here it is a typist with perfect stamina: 1,250 auction listings written from photographs, 400+ hours returned to a real team, every listing checked by a person. The machine does volume; people do judgement.
