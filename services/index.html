The short answer

What does a growth agency in Karachi do?

Drifted is a growth agency for ads, content and AI apps, founded 2026 in Karachi and working New York hours. We run Meta and Google ads, make the content that feeds them, and build custom AI apps — one team and one monthly fee, with one number and one date written into the contract.

Performance media
Paid advertising on Meta (Instagram and Facebook) and Google, where the creative, the campaign structure and the budget are run by one team and judged against one agreed number — revenue, orders or cost per customer — by one agreed date.
Creator growth
Short-form video, carousels and posts made for the stranger scrolling past rather than the follower who already knows the brand — and, for creators, turning an audience into a business with products priced to margin before anything is designed.
AI product
Custom AI apps built for one business's real workload — for example writing auction listings from photographs — scoped against how the tool saves time or makes money before any code is written, and checked by a person where judgement matters.
